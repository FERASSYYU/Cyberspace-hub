# Atmail eligibility flag (unresolved)

The published Atmail policy (https://www.atmail.com/bug-bounty-terms/, read 2026-11-18) contains
an inconsistency that must be resolved in writing before any live research:

- Eligibility: researchers under 18 may participate with parent or guardian consent, and any reward goes to that parent or guardian.
- Payments: rewards are paid only to the verified individual who submitted the report, never to third parties.

Status: **unresolved**. No live research and no payment assumptions are made until the researcher and
parent or legal guardian obtain written clarification from Atmail (security@atmail.com).

PBH does not contact any Atmail host. The Atmail profile is reference only, with live testing disabled.
